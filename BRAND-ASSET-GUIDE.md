# LOCOXATL — Brand asset integration

## Asset review
The supplied `LOCOXATL.zip` contains seven 1080 × 1080 PNG brand explorations. It does not contain product photography, keychain cut-outs, or individual charm renders.

## Applied in the website

- `assets/brand/locoxatl-wordmark-black.png` — cropped, transparent primary horizontal wordmark derived from supplied `1.png`; used in the main header and footer.
- `assets/brand/locoxatl-wordmark-white.png` — white reverse version of the same wordmark; used as a subtle watermark on the black closing section.
- `assets/brand/lxl-mark-black.png` — cropped, transparent LXL mark derived from supplied `4.png`; used in the compact product navigation and brand-story panel.
- `assets/brand/favicon.png` — square browser icon composed from the LXL mark.
- `assets/brand/locoxatl-symbol-black.png` and `locoxatl-symbol-white.png` — transparent variants derived from supplied `5.png`, retained for future use.
- `assets/brand/source-assets/` — all seven original team PNGs, preserved unchanged.

## Visual system

- Typography: Inter, served locally from `assets/fonts/`.
- Core colors: pure black `#000000` and pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Supporting neutrals: neutral grayscale only; no additional brand hue has been introduced.
- Existing product illustrations and builder preview remain monochrome SVG mockups because no product-specific photography or transparent product renders were included in the supplied archive.

## Asset usage notes

Use the horizontal LOCOXATL wordmark for primary brand identification. Use the LXL mark as a secondary compact identifier. The alternate lockups in `source-assets/6.png` and `source-assets/7.png` are preserved but are not mixed into the primary interface, to avoid competing logo styles.
